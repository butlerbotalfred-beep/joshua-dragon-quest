# Dragon Quest Story and Dialogue Draft

Nov 22, 2026 · @Michael Buck

## The story in one paragraph

Long ago the Keeper of the Forest hid four dragon eggs in a secret grove, because a baby dragon grows up to be like whoever loves it first. He asked his own wife to guard them. She is Renee, the Ward, a kindly older woman who has lived at the castle for years as Joshua and Daniel's tutor, watching over the eggs and the royal family she has come to love. The Keeper stayed behind to tend the forest, and the two have lived apart ever since. The Great Black Dragon is proud and lonely, and she wants a family that will follow her. On the morning of the Spring Mountain Festival she steals the eggs and lands on the royal castle. The Ward acts at once and flings the eggs to four corners of the kingdom, out of her reach, where they will soon hatch. Furious, the dragon puts the forest's gentle guardians under a spell and sends them to fetch the eggs back. Renee stays at the castle to keep an eye on the dragon, and sends word of everything she does to the Keeper. The royal family is out picking berries when it happens. The Keeper comes to them in person and tells them the story of the dragon. He gives each of them a gift: speed for Joshua, fire for Daniel, ice for Vanessa and strength for Michael. If they prove themselves worthy, he will make the gifts theirs for good. His first task is to get out of the woods and free the Great Bear. They free each guardian, reach each egg as it glows brighter, and are the first faces the hatchlings see. Beneath the castle, the four little dragons show the Great Black Dragon the kindness they were shown. Her anger gives out, the family forgives her, and she stays for the festival and becomes the Protector of the Kingdom. At the festival the Keeper keeps his promise. With the eggs safe at last, he and the Ward can live together again.

How this closes the six loose threads from the audit:

- **Why the eggs are scattered.** She did not throw them. The Ward did, to keep them from her. The Ward is the boys' own tutor and the Keeper's wife.
- **Good or blank.** Neither. Hatchlings become like whoever loves them, so who reaches the egg first truly matters.
- **Where the powers come from.** They are the Keeper's gifts, given in person at the start and made permanent if the family proves worthy.
- **What she wants.** A family, taken the wrong way. That is why she goes for eggs and not for the Keeper.
- **The race.** It is felt in the story only. Eggs glow and wobble as you near them, and nobody can arrive too late.
- **The ending.** Nobody takes a mother's children, and nobody is driven away.

Changed on November 23 at Michael's direction. The Keeper appears in person right after the dragon lands, tells the story of the dragon, gives the gifts and sets the first task. The Ward is no longer an old enchantment. The Ward is a person the family already knows. She is Renee, the Keeper's wife, a kindly older woman. She waits at the castle to keep an eye on the dragon, and every update the Keeper gives the family about the dragon comes from her. The two are reunited at the festival. One change this forces in the game: the Keeper needs a prologue scene before Level 1, since at present the family first meets him after the bear.

## Level by level

Every line below is meant to be spoken, and none is longer than a six-year-old can follow. The family's lines are placeholders in a plain, warm voice until you fill in the four voice cards. Each level lists one moment per family member, to honor the rule that every level needs everyone.

### Prologue: festival morning

The family is picking berries when the dragon lands on the castle and the eggs streak across the sky. The Keeper steps out of the trees, tells them the story of the dragon, gives the four gifts and sets the first task.

- **Joshua:** "Dad, look! Shooting stars! In the daytime!"
- **Great Black Dragon:** "MY eggs! Come back here this instant! Oh, bother."
- **Keeper, stepping out of the trees:** "Do not be afraid, little family. I am the Keeper of the Forest."
- **Keeper:** "That dragon is proud, and she is lonely. She took four eggs to make a family that must follow her."
- **Keeper:** "Your tutor, Renee, is my Ward, and my wife. She threw the eggs far away to keep them safe. Now they need you."
- **Daniel:** "Our teacher did that?"
- **Keeper:** "Hold out your hands. Speed for Joshua. Fire for Daniel. Ice for Vanessa. Strength for Michael."
- **Keeper:** "Prove yourselves worthy, and these gifts are yours to keep. First, find your way out of these woods and free the Great Bear."
- **Michael:** "Then we had better make it a good day. Stay close, boys."

### Level 1: The Great Woods

The family learns their gifts and how to help each other. The guardian is the Great Bear, who is grumpy only because of the spell.

- **Each one's moment:** Daniel burns the brambles. Michael moves the boulder. Vanessa freezes the stream to cross. Joshua outruns the bear's charge.
- **Hero in a web:** "I'm stuck! Somebody swap in! Hee hee, stop it!"
- **Keeper, as a hint:** "A web is too sticky for one. Call your family."
- **Vanessa, freeing someone:** "Got you. Up you come."
- **Keeper, when the bear is freed:** "There. The spell is gone. He was never angry at you."
- **Daniel:** "He's smiling! Can a bear smile?"
- **Keeper, when they meet again:** "Well done. Renee sends word from the castle. The dragon is searching for the eggs. A baby dragon grows up to be like whoever loves it first. Reach each one before her spell does."

### Level 2: Treetop Canopy, the green egg

High walkways and swinging platforms. The guardian is the Old Vine, tangled up in the spell.

- **Each one's moment:** Daniel clears the overgrowth. Michael holds a bridge steady. Vanessa freezes a waterfall into steps. Joshua makes the last long leap to the nest.
- **Keeper, near the egg:** "See how it glows? It can hear you coming, Joshua."
- **Joshua, as it hatches:** "Hi. I'm Joshua. I'll look after you. I promise."
- **Speedy:** a happy chirp, then a zoom around Joshua's head.
- **Michael:** "I think you have a friend for life, son."

### Level 3: Scorched Caverns, the red egg

Warm caves and glowing rock. The guardian is the Ember Golem, who cannot cool down.

- **Each one's moment:** Joshua and Speedy race up the steep ash slope. Vanessa cools the vents. Michael lifts fallen rock. Daniel lights the dark tunnel to the nest.
- **Daniel:** "It's so hot in here. Mommy, can you make it snow?"
- **Keeper, when the golem is freed:** "Feel that? He is only warm now, like a hearth. Renee tells me the dragon knows two eggs have hatched. Hurry on."
- **Daniel, as it hatches:** "You're warm. Hello, Ember. I'm Daniel. I've got you."
- **Joshua:** "Now we both have one! Speedy, say hi!"

### Level 4: Frostpeak Ridge, the blue egg

Snow, ice walls and a frozen river. The guardian is the Yeti, who only wanted quiet.

- **Each one's moment:** Daniel and Ember melt the ice walls. Joshua and Speedy fetch a key across thin ice. Michael clears a snowdrift. Vanessa calms the storm at the nest.
- **Michael:** "Everyone warm enough? Daniel, stay near your mother."
- **Keeper, as a hint:** "That ice is too thick for a small flame. Ask Ember to help."
- **Vanessa, as it hatches:** "Hello, little one. I'm Vanessa. You're safe with us."
- **Daniel:** "Mommy has a dragon! Mommy has a dragon!"

### Level 5: The Craglands, the purple egg

Old stone ruins and heavy doors. The guardian is the Stone Gargoyle, who has stood watch too long.

- **Each one's moment:** Vanessa and Frosty freeze a bridge over the chasm. Joshua and Speedy climb the steep ramp to drop a ladder. Daniel and Ember light the old lamps to open a door. Michael pushes the great stone block.
- **Joshua:** "Three dragons. One more. Come on, Dad, it's your turn!"
- **Keeper, when the gargoyle is freed:** "Rest now, old friend. Your watch is over."
- **Michael, as it hatches:** "Well, hello there. I'm Michael. Strong little fellow, aren't you?"
- **Vanessa:** "Four eggs, four dragons. Now let's go home."

### Level 6: beneath the castle

Renee meets the family at the castle door and shows them the way down. All four gifts and all four dragons are needed, one after another, to reach the dragon.

- **Each one's moment:** Michael and Samson open the stone gate. Vanessa and Frosty bridge the river. Daniel and Ember melt the ice seal. Joshua and Speedy dash through the closing door to let the others in.
- **Great Black Dragon:** "Those are MY dragons! They were going to follow ME!"
- **Joshua:** "They don't follow us. They're our friends."
- **Keeper, when her anger runs out:** "Look at them. They are not afraid of you. They never were."
- **Great Black Dragon:** "I only wanted a family of my own, I only wanted somewhere to live where I feel loved and wanted."
- **Vanessa:** "You can't force people to love you, but you can choose to be kind, you can choose how to treat others, and in return you'll find love, friends, and maybe a family of your own."
- **Great Black Dragon:** "I don't think anyone could love me."
- **Vanessa:** "Have you tried being kind to others? Why don't you stay? Come to the festival, meet the people who live here, spend time with the baby dragons, you might realize you can have everything you want when you treat others with kindness and respect."

### The festival

The courtyard fills with lanterns, one pair for every gold berry found. The bear, the yeti and the other guardians are there, and so is she.

- **Keeper:** "You have proved yourselves worthy. The gifts are yours to keep. But your best gift was always each other."
- **Daniel:** "Can the dragons stay?"
- **Keeper:** "Ask them."
- **All four dragons:** a chorus of chirps.
- **Renee, to the Keeper:** "The eggs and the family are safe, my dear. It's time for me to come home."
- **Keeper:** "I have missed you, my love." They embrace.
- **Great Black Dragon:** "Hmph. It is a rather nice festival."
- **Narrator:** "And so the Dragon did just that. She stayed for the festival, she laughed for the first time in her life and realized how beautiful life can be when we look for the good in the world instead of the darkness. And in the end, she found the family she had been looking for and became the Protector of the Kingdom, loved by everyone, and they all lived happily ever after."
